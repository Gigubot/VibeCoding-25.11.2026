❓ How to play

  • Volcano: pick a contrast, move the sliders or drag the dashed lines. Counts and the plain-English direction update live.
  • 🪤 LOD Trap: clicking a purple × (an estimate with lod_imputed) asks what the flag means before the card opens.
  • 📉 Meter: with Benjamini–Hochberg at cutoff q, up to q × (number of hits) of your hits are expected to be false. Loosen past 0.25 and come back to ≤ 0.1 for a badge.
  • 🎯 Sign Sniper: decide which group has more. After a 5-answer streak, interaction questions appear: Rg = log2(SNg) − log2(TSBg).
  • 🕵️ Mystery: fewer clues = more XP. 🧬 Family Hunt: catch every family member that passes your cutoffs.
  • 📖 Pokédex: every opened protein is collected; 🧊 = AlphaFold structure viewed. AlphaFold models are NCTC8325 reference proteins, so JE2 residue numbers can differ.
  • Plot per-sample values before trusting a fold change. Missing values are NA, never 0. PASN is spent medium, not co-culture. The stp mutant also has less Stk1.
