❓ How to play

  • Volcano: pick one of the contrasts (grouped by type), move the sliders or drag the dashed lines. Use the Show on plot switches to hide or show up/down hits, non-selected proteins and LOD-imputed estimates.
  • 🪤 LOD Trap: clicking a purple × asks what lod_imputed means before the card opens.
  • 📉 Meter: with Benjamini–Hochberg at cutoff q, up to q × (number of hits) of your hits are expected to be false. Loosen past 0.25 and come back to ≤ 0.1 for a badge.
  • 🎯 Sign Sniper: 3 lives, 8 s per shot, combos multiply points, wrong or slow answers cost XP. Interactions (Rg = log2(SNg) − log2(TSBg)) unlock at combo ×4.
  • 🌋 Volcano Eruption: no biology knowledge needed. Proteins erupt and land at their real position on the volcano plot of the current contrast. Click the ones in the coloured corners (significant and big change), leave the grey middle, never click a purple ✕. Every 100 points the cutoffs change.
  • 🗺️ Pathway Explorer: category → pathway → protein → AlphaFold 3D model. Pathways come from UniProt (NCTC8325 reference proteome). The chosen pathway is highlighted on the volcano.
  • 📖 Pokédex: every opened protein is collected; 🧊 = structure viewed; 👑 = Stk1/Stp1. AlphaFold models are NCTC8325 reference proteins, so JE2 residue numbers can differ.
  • 🔊 Sounds can be muted with the speaker button in the header. Plot per-sample values before trusting a fold change; missing values are NA, never 0. PASN is spent medium, not co-culture. The stp mutant also has less Stk1.
